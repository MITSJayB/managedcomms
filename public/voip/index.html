The PSTN switch-off: act before 31 January 2027

The UK’s analogue phone network is due to close on 31 January 2027, and ISDN lines are closing with it. If your business still relies on either, you need a plan now. In September 2026, Openreach counted about 1.2 million lines still to move, including about 350,000 business premises.

What happens if you don’t move?

Openreach says there won’t be a “big bang” on 1 February, but that isn’t a reason to wait. Lines that haven’t moved may go onto Emergency Voice Access, a temporary, last-resort service for essential calls. It costs more than a modern digital line, and Openreach’s wholesale charge for a basic line becomes £35 a month, before any supplier adds its own margin. Other lines may be disconnected, and some suppliers have already set earlier cut-offs.

It’s not only your phones

The equipment most easily missed is often the most important. Check anything that uses a phone line, including:

  • Lift emergency phones and alarm signalling
  • Door entry and building management systems
  • Card terminals and cash machines
  • CCTV links, lone-worker call points and telemetry
  • Older phone systems, ISDN lines, overflow routes and rarely used numbers
  • Older broadband that depends on the phone line

What to do now

  1. List every line and number. Include inbound numbers, backup routes and connected equipment.
  2. Find out who owns each one. Confirm the supplier, any cut-off date and who is responsible internally.
  3. Don’t cancel lines that look unused. Check first that nothing depends on them.
  4. Move your numbers first. Transfer them before the old service is ceased.
  5. Test before you switch over. Check call routing, card payments, alarms and emergency calling, including during a power cut.

Questions we often get

Can I keep my phone numbers?

In most cases, yes. We’ll check your numbers and manage the transfer as part of your move.

Do I need new handsets?

Not necessarily. Your team can use an app on a computer or mobile, and we’ll check whether your current handsets are compatible.

Will my internet connection cope?

We’ll check before you switch. If your connection isn’t up to it, we’ll recommend an upgrade so call quality isn’t left to chance.

What happens if our internet goes down?

Calls can be diverted to mobiles or another location, and a backup connection can keep you running. We’ll build the right plan into your setup.
