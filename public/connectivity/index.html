Leased lines: a connection that’s yours alone

A leased line is a private, dedicated fibre connection between your premises and the network. You don’t share it with neighbouring businesses, so your speeds stay more consistent when the area gets busy.

It’s often worth considering if you:

  • Have a large team working in cloud apps all day
  • Run lots of VoIP calls or video meetings at once
  • Regularly upload large files or back up offsite
  • Connect several sites or host your own systems
  • Lose real money whenever the internet goes down

Plan for when things go wrong

Even the best connection can fail. We’ll look at what would stop your business working and suggest the right backup, such as a second line or a 4G or 5G failover that takes over if your main connection drops.

Questions we often get

How long does a leased line take to install?

It varies by location and the work required, and it usually takes longer than broadband. We’ll give you an estimated timescale with your quote.

Do I need a leased line, or will full fibre do?

Full fibre suits many smaller businesses very well. A leased line makes more sense when consistent speed and fast fault response matter more than the lower monthly cost.

Will the PSTN switch-off affect my broadband?

It can. Older broadband services that rely on the copper phone network may need to move, so we’ll check your current lines as part of your review.
